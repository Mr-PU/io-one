Why read this book?

Four questions worth sitting with.

Who are you?

Beyond your name, roles, memories, and beliefs, what remains?

What is identity?

How much of the person you call “you” was consciously chosen?

Where does separation begin?

What creates the boundary between the self and the world?

What if “one” is an illusion?

A question at the heart of the journey explored in this book.

A journey inward

This is not a book that asks you to accept a particular answer.

It asks you to pause. To question what feels obvious.

And perhaps, in questioning what we believe ourselves to be, to discover a different way of seeing what it means to exist.
